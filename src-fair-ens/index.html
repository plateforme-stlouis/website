FAIR Ensemble: monthly meeting series where we, bioinformatics, chat.

  • Why? Our goal is to connect the community and share practical approaches that make research more FAIR and workflows more reliable.
  • Who? Anyone interested. Feel free to join! Let's foster interactions and skill sharing.
  • When? The first Monday of every month at 2:00 PM.
  • How? A 30-minute presentation followed by an interactive Q&A.
  • Where? The location is confirmed ahead of the session.
    • Salle du Conseil: Main entrance of Hayem building; Second floor; Right; Room facing the entrance.
    • Salle Laurent Degos: Main entrance of Hayem building; Basement; Right; Room on the left
    • Salle Orchidée: Main entrance of Hayem building; First floor; Right; Corridor red & green; Room on the left, halfway.
